# Code of Conduct

Tactara is a small fan project made for fun. Everyone who reports a bug, suggests a rotation or sends a change should feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background or favorite class.

## Be good to each other

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to addons or to GitHub.
- Assume good intent. Most rough-sounding comments are just short ones.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. "This step casts Rend too often" is helpful; "you don't know how to play" isn't.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into Tactara, and that "not right now" isn't personal.

## Not okay here

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Spam, ads, or selling anything.
- Sharing bots, automation, exploits or anything else that breaks Blizzard's rules. Tactara stays an assistant: every cast comes from the player's own key press.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## If something goes wrong

If someone breaks these rules, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or open an issue titled "Conduct" (no details needed) and the maintainer, Zudryx, will follow up privately.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who don't follow these rules.
